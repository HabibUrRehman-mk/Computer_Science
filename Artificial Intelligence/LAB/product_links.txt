Customers
(customer_id, name, phone, email, address, city, type [retail/contract])

Suppliers
(supplier_id, name, contact_person, phone, email, address, payment_terms)


3. Inventory & Raw Materials

MaterialTypes
(type_id, type_name [Aluminium, Glass, Screw, Glue, Rubber, Tools])

RawMaterials
(material_id, name, type_id, unit, size, thickness, colour, current_stock, reorder_level, supplier_id)

InventoryTransactions
(transaction_id, material_id, qty, transaction_type [in/out], date, linked_task_id, purchase_invoice_id)

ToolsMachines
(tool_id, name, type, status [available/in use/maintenance], purchase_date, last_service_date)

Quotations
(quotation_id, customer_id, created_by, date, status [pending/approved/rejected], total_price, notes)

(order_id, quotation_id, customer_id, created_by, order_date, status [in-progress/completed/cancelled])

(invoice_id, order_id, customer_id, issue_date, due_date, total_amount, status [paid/unpaid/partial])

(invoice_item_id, invoice_id, material_id, description, qty, unit_price, total)

(purchase_item_id, purchase_invoice_id, material_id, qty, unit_price, total)

(payment_id, invoice_id, amount, method [cash/bank/cheque/online], date, received_by)

Expenses
(expense_id, category, description, amount, date, added_by)


🔹 Database Workflow (How Data Flows)
Step 1: Quotation → Sales Order

Sales staff creates Quotation → Customer accepts → Becomes SalesOrder.

OrderItems reserved from RawMaterials stock.

Step 2: Production Workflow

Supervisor assigns ProductionTasks (Cutting, Colouring, Assembly, etc.).

Workers update status → logged in TaskLogs.

Materials consumed → recorded in InventoryTransactions.

Step 3: Delivery & Installation

Once order is complete → linked to DeliveryInstallation.

Team assigned, delivery scheduled, customer signs off.

Step 4: Billing & Accounting

System generates Invoice from SalesOrder.

Customer pays → recorded in Payments.

Raw materials purchased → recorded in PurchaseInvoices & PurchaseInvoiceItems.

Expenses (transport, labour) logged in Expenses.

Step 5: Reporting & Decision Making

InventoryTransactions + Invoices = Profitability reports.

ProductionTasks + TaskLogs = Employee performance.

Customers + SalesOrders = Sales trends.

Suppliers + PurchaseInvoices = Supplier performance.
